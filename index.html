How we hire

Five tests every leader meets. Five steps to find out.

  • Walk-in

    The room adjusts when they enter, with no title announcement needed.

  • Reach

    Peers, regulators, institutions and partners take their call.

  • Trust

    Former colleagues, vendors and rivals would describe them as fair.

  • Second meeting

    People ask to meet them again.

  • Silence

    They can hold a room by listening.

  1. Apply

    One short application. Tell us who you are and which seat you want.

  2. Talk

    A first conversation about your background and the role.

  3. Go deep

    A working session on the real problems of the seat.

  4. Meet leadership

    Time with the Founder and Director or the hiring leader before the final stage.

  5. Decide

    We tell you the outcome either way, with one specific reason.

Every seat comes with a written mandate: what you own, what success looks like in 12 months, and how you are measured.
